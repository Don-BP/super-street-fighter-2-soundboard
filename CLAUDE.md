# Super Street Fighter 2 Soundboard

## What it is
A plain website of buttons that play the sounds from Super Street Fighter 2. It runs in the browser (phones included). No database, no logins.

## Who it is for
Don and his friends. It is shared by link. It is not a business product.

## Accounts and ownership
- GitHub: `Don-BP` — repo `Don-BP/super-street-fighter-2-soundboard` (public).
- Hosting: GitHub Pages, served from the `main` branch of that same repo. One repo, one host, one account — do not split it.
- Live site: https://don-bp.github.io/super-street-fighter-2-soundboard/
- No database, no other paid or third-party services.

## Heads-up on the sounds
The game sounds belong to Capcom. The repo and site are public (GitHub Pages needs that on a free account), so a takedown request is possible. Nothing is sold and there is no advertising.
